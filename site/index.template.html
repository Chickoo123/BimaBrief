01 / PREMIUM & PAYOUT“₹70 paid per ₹100 premium means 70% of claims were approved.”Unpack this ↘
NOT THE SAME THING

This compares amounts paid and premium reported in the same financial year. Claims paid may relate to policies from earlier years. It is not an approval rate.

02 / INCURRED CLAIMS“A high health ICR proves an insurer serves customers better.”Unpack this ↘
ONE SIGNAL, NOT A SCORE

ICR compares incurred claims with earned premium for the specified business. It does not measure service quality, solvency or your personal approval chance.

03 / COMPLAINTS“More complaints automatically means a worse insurer.”Unpack this ↘
CHECK THE DENOMINATOR

Complaint totals depend on business size, product mix and reporting definitions. If complaint scope and policy denominator differ, the resulting rate is only a rough indicator—not an exact health-complaint rate.

Cashless authorisation timelines

WAITING PERIODS

Pre-existing disease waiting period

The 2024 health-insurance circular sets a maximum waiting period of 36 months for pre-existing diseases. The waiting period for a specific plan may be shorter; check its policy wording and any later amendment.

Find official health product documents ↗
CONTINUOUS COVER

Moratorium is not a claim guarantee

After 60 continuous months, contesting a policy or claim on non-disclosure or misrepresentation grounds is restricted, except in cases of established fraud. Policy exclusions, limits, co-payments and other applicable terms do not simply disappear; enhanced cover may be treated separately.

Verify the applicable circular ↗
RENEWAL

A previous claim is not, by itself, the whole answer

The 2024 circular includes a protection against denying renewal solely because claims were made in previous policy years. Always verify the complete applicable rule, permitted exceptions and the policy's actual terms.

Review official notices and circulars ↗
ENTRY AGE

No blanket upper-age bar

The 2024 health-insurance circular provides for health products across age groups, without a blanket upper age limit for entry. This does not mean every product has identical benefits or that every proposal is automatically accepted; verify the specific product and current rules.

08 / SOURCES & METHODOpen and inspectable

About the data

Every result should answer three things: where it came from, how it was calculated, and what it cannot prove.

⌕
Why I built BimaBrief

I started BimaBrief because official insurance statistics can be difficult to explore and easy to misread. I wanted a practical public tool that links each result to its source, explains the calculation and says clearly when the available data cannot answer a question. The aim is to make the evidence easier to understand—not to attack or endorse any company.

— Harshil Desai, Project Lead

Where the numbers come from

Figures come from the IRDAI Handbook on Indian Insurance Statistics 2024–25, including Table 67 (state-wise health insurance business), Table 72 (state-wise claim settlement), Tables 53, 56 and 58 (insurer claims, grievances and policies), Table 62 (health incurred claims ratio), Table 49 (solvency), Table 80 (network providers) and Table 66 (industry claims development and aging). Health business in these tables excludes personal accident and travel unless noted otherwise. The Handbook is published annually.

What “claims paid per ₹100 of premium” means

It compares the amount of claims paid in a financial year with the premium reported in the same year. Some claims paid may relate to policies bought earlier. It is not the official Incurred Claims Ratio, not the Claim Settlement Ratio, not a measure of profit and not the probability that an individual claim will be paid.

Trends show what happened, not why. The data alone does not establish the cause of a change.

Own policy, employer or group, and government schemes

Own policy means an individual or family policy. Employer or group means cover provided through a group, such as employees. Government schemes are government-backed programmes. These categories have different funding and coverage structures and should not be treated as interchangeable.

What “Not available” means

“Not available” means the source did not provide a usable value or a fair calculation cannot be made. It does not mean zero. Source-reported zeroes and missing values are preserved separately.

Why some comparisons are limited or blocked

Metrics are compared only when their definitions, time period, units, denominators and insurance scope are compatible. A whole-company metric is not automatically health-only. The network-provider table is a 2024–25 snapshot, not a ten-year series.

How data discrepancies are handled

Automated checks validate headers, units, totals and key relationships. If IRDAI’s own table does not reconcile under a documented check, the values are preserved and the discrepancy is flagged rather than silently corrected. Every build runs tests; passing tests confirms only the checks defined, not that every possible error has been eliminated.
