# Data source, attribution and reuse notice

BimaBrief uses public insurance statistics published by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI). The current public snapshot is derived from the IRDAI *Handbook on Indian Insurance Statistics* (2024–25). The website provides source-table references and calculation limits. Official source: https://irdai.gov.in/handbook-of-indian-insurance

The public repository intentionally does **not** redistribute complete copies of the official Excel workbooks because their applicable redistribution/reuse terms have not been verified. Maintainers should download workbooks directly from IRDAI for local processing, follow the source terms and attribution requirements, and keep raw workbook copies out of public Git history. Published JSON snapshots under `data/published/` are derived outputs used by the site; this note does not grant any separate right to redistribute underlying source material beyond applicable law and source terms.
