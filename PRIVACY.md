# Privacy note

BimaBrief's static data explorer does not require accounts and does not collect claim experiences or policyholder submissions.

## Optional AI Policy Fit Checker

The AI comparison feature is disabled until a separate secure backend is configured. If enabled, PDF files selected by a user are sent by that backend to the AI provider selected by the user. The application is designed not to persist the uploaded files, but the selected provider will process the document content under its own applicable terms and retention policies. Never assume that a document is private from the provider merely because BimaBrief does not save it.

Before uploading, users should remove names, contact details, policy numbers, claim references, addresses, medical records and other sensitive information where possible. Do not upload documents unless you have authority to share them with the selected provider. The feature is for comparing the evidence in documents; it does not give a claim approval prediction or a binding recommendation.

The site is not an official IRDAI service and is not a channel for filing insurance grievances. Use [Bima Bharosa](https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/) for official grievance handling.
